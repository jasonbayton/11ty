**Features & improvements**

- Remove **Open system settings** from the custom intent type dropdown and **System action** from the managed-configuration form. Use **Launch activity** with the required Android or device manufacturer action for custom settings shortcuts.

Existing `system` entries and `systemAction` values remain supported in policy JSON. The change affects the configuration form only. See [Configure custom intents](/projects/managed-settings/support/custom-intents/).

N/A

**Known issues**

Some OEMs/devices do not support AOSP settings intents. APN settings can also return or fail inside the device's Settings app after a launch is accepted.

OEM/device support and the reporting process are documented under [OEM support](/projects/managed-settings/support/oem-support/).
