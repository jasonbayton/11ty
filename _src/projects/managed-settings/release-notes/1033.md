**Features & improvements**

- Add diagnostic reporting for crashes and failed settings or custom actions to help investigate device-specific issues. Reports include app and device details, the failed operation and a stack trace. Intent extras and URI values are excluded.
- Queue reports locally when the device is offline and retry when connectivity is available. Reporting starts after the first device unlock following a restart.

**Fixes**

- Fix a crash caused by tapping the configuration menu repeatedly while it was opening.

**Known issues**

Some OEMs/devices do not support AOSP settings intents. APN settings can also return or fail inside the device's Settings app after a launch is accepted.

OEM/device support and the reporting process are documented under [OEM support](/projects/managed-settings/support/oem-support/).
