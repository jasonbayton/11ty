**Fixes**

- Reject custom activity and broadcast entries that specify a class name without a package name. Previously, the class could be ignored and the remaining action sent as an implicit intent.

See [Configure custom intents](/projects/managed-settings/support/custom-intents/).

**Known issues**

Some OEMs/devices do not support AOSP settings intents. APN settings can also return or fail inside the device's Settings app after a launch is accepted.

OEM/device support and the reporting process are documented under [OEM support](/projects/managed-settings/support/oem-support/).
