_Covers releases 1.3.0 to 1.3.1._

**Features & improvements**

- **Build-screen feedback link.** The Studio build success screen now links directly to the feedback form for reporting bugs, requesting features or asking questions.

**Fixes**

- **Reliable self-signing aliases.** Leading and trailing whitespace is removed from self-signing key aliases so valid keystore aliases do not fail during the build. Passwords are unchanged because their whitespace can be intentional.
