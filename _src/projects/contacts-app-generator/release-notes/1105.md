_Covers releases 1.1.0 to 1.1.5._

**Features & improvements**

- Upload fields now state their maximum size, so the limit is visible before a build is rejected.

**Fixes**

- **Prompt managed-configuration updates.** When `sync_on_config_change` is enabled and the app is running, a managed-configuration push now re-syncs promptly and refreshes the open contact list, branding and status in place, where previously the change was not delivered. Wi-Fi-only sync settings continue to apply.
- **Safer uploads.** Icon, keystore and source-file uploads over their size limits are rejected rather than being accepted as truncated files that could produce a corrupt app.
- **Security settings fail safely.** Invalid rate-limit settings now stop startup rather than silently disabling rate limiting, and the alerting token is not sent to non-HTTPS servers, except permitted loopback HTTP addresses.
